TOP OF MY SCHOOL

Remote Session-Cellist

TOP OF MY SCHOOL is a multi-award-winning short film directed by Katherine Lynn-Rose.

For this self-recorded session, I recorded my cello playing using Pro Tools as my DAW. I used the Neumann KM184 condenser microphones to produce a warm yet detailed sound. I focused on placing the microphones in a way that captures the dynamic changes while keeping the right tone that serves the orchestration.
